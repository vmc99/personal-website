Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2025

Overview

This privacy policy describes how I collect, use, and protect information when you visit mvamsi.com. Your privacy is important to me.

What I Collect

I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site. This includes basic info like pages visited, time spent, and general location (country/city level). No personal information is collected.

How I Use It

Analytics help me improve the site and understand what content is useful. If you email me directly, I'll only use your contact info to respond. I never sell or share any information.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to track visits. You can disable cookies in your browser if you prefer.

Your Rights

You can request information about data I collect, ask for it to be deleted, or opt out of analytics. Just email me with any privacy concerns.
