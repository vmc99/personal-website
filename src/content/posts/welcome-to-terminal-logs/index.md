Terminal Logs is where I write down what I am building and what I learn on the way: software engineering, AI, the tools I use every day, and anything else I find interesting enough to work through in writing.

## What goes here

Mostly three kinds of entry. Notes from projects, including the decisions and the dead ends. Fixes for problems I have actually hit, written up so the next person (often me) finds them faster. And longer pieces on ideas I want to understand properly before I hold an opinion on them.

## Why "Terminal Logs"

The terminal is where I first understood how systems really work: abstract ideas become concrete commands, and problems get solved one line at a time. A log is the honest record of that, kept as you go. These are mine.

If something here is useful, or wrong, I would like to hear about it. My other work is on the [portfolio](/).
